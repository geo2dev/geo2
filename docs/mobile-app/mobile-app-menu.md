# Mobile App: Menu

## Introduction

Press the `menu` icon at the top-left corner of Map page in the mobile app to open the menu. If you have permissions, the menu includes the following options:

- Ask AI assistant
- Plan a route
- Create a POD (Proof of delivery)
- Create a vehicle check
- Check routes list
- Record mileage tracking without a planned route
- Review mileage tracking records and export a report
- Check PODs history
- Check vehicle checks history
- Share Advanced with friends
- Review pending uploads for your PODs and vehicle checks
- Configure settings
- Change organization and environment if you have multiple
- Access profile information

## Ask AI Assistant

The Geo2 AI assistant helps you manage your daily routes, stops, and deliveries with fewer manual actions. It is particularly useful when you need to adjust plans on the go, add new stops, check delivery progress, retrieve delivery information, or quickly find your way around the mobile app.

You can interact with the assistant using text, voice input, or hands-free Voice Mode. In Voice Mode, the assistant listens to your requests and responds verbally, allowing you to manage routes, check delivery details, and receive guidance without typing.

You can also continue the conversation while navigating between different screens in the app. The assistant remembers the context of your current conversation, so you can ask follow-up questions without repeating information.

With the AI assistant, you can:

- Create new routes and update existing ones
- Add stops using addresses, place names, or voice input
- Import addresses and delivery details from photos, screenshots, and PDFs
- Move individual, multiple, or undelivered stops between routes
- Change the order of stops and breaks within a route
- Optimize routes with or without required stop time windows
- Add, edit, and delete breaks
- Assign drivers to routes
- Start and complete routes
- Find routes planned for specific dates or assigned to specific drivers
- Check route progress, remaining stops, and estimated completion times
- Retrieve stop details and delivery instructions
- Check delivery statuses and find completed, pending, failed, or undelivered stops
- Retrieve Proof of Delivery (POD) information, including delivery status, recorded date and time, driver details, and the availability of photos and signatures
- Navigate to your next stop or open specific routes, stops, orders, and other areas of the app
- Get step-by-step guidance on using different Geo2 features

**Use cases:**

1. If you receive a list of delivery addresses by message or email, you can ask the assistant to create a route from them using text, voice input, or by attaching photos, screenshots, or PDFs instead of entering each address manually.

2. If a new urgent delivery comes up during the day, you can ask the assistant to add it to your current route and re-optimize the stop order.

3. When certain stops must be visited within specific time windows, you can ask the assistant to optimize your route while considering these requirements.

4. If you need to reschedule unsuccessful deliveries, you can ask the assistant to move all undelivered stops to a new route planned for tomorrow.

5. If you want to check your progress during the day, you can ask how many stops you have left, what your next delivery is, or what time you are expected to finish your route.

6. If a customer asks about their delivery, you can ask the assistant to check whether the order has been delivered, when it was delivered, who completed the delivery, and whether POD photos or a signature are available.

7. If your plans change, you can ask the assistant to prioritize a specific stop, move several stops to the end of your route, or reschedule your break.

8. If you need to check delivery instructions before arriving at your next stop, you can ask the assistant to read them aloud using Voice Mode.

9. If you are unsure how to use a feature, you can ask the assistant for step-by-step instructions directly in the app.

The available AI assistant capabilities and usage limits depend on your subscription level and assigned user permissions.

## Routes

Press the `Routes` option to open Routes Calendar page. It lets you view routes that have been released to you, each displayed with a small map preview. By default, routes planned for the current date are shown, but you can select other dates to show routes for those dates. The green dot below the date means that you have planned routes assigned to you for this date. Learn more about [Routes and Stops](https://help.geo2.com/mobile-app/mobile-app-routes-and-stops).

In the mobile app, you can view routes from the past 30 days and the next 30 days. To access the full routes history, you can visit **[Geo2 web-based Hub](https://hub.geo2.com/en-GB/auth/signin)**. Data older than 30 days is available with Pro, Advanced, or Enterprise subscription.

![20261124_9.png](/attachments/mwuq9g4p-5ckzyh2u.png)

## Mileage Tracking

Press the `Mileage tracking` option to open the Mileage tracking page in the mobile app. It lets you record, review, and manage your actual mileage for business, commuting, or personal journeys. Mileage is automatically recorded when you execute a planned route, but you can also start tracking a journey without creating a route or adding stops.

The page displays your total mileage, the number of completed routes, and individual mileage records for the selected period. You can filter your records by date range, vehicles, and purpose codes. You can also export a mileage report in PDF format for record-keeping and expense reporting.

Background location tracking permission is required to ensure accurate mileage recording. Learn more about [Mileage tracking](https://help.geo2.com/mobile-app/mobile-app-mileage-tracking).

## PODs

In the mobile app, you can view PODs history from the past 30 days. To access the full PODs history, you can visit **[Geo2 web-based Hub](https://hub.geo2.com/en-GB/auth/signin)**. Data older than 30 days is available with Pro, Advanced, or Enterprise subscription.

## Pending Uploads

If you have a bad internet connection while uploading pictures / signatures to the app for a POD or vehicle check and it takes more time to upload them, you can find them on `Pending uploads` page in Menu. Once an internet connection is good again, they will be uploaded automatically. Alternatively, you can click on them to push photos / signatures one more time.

## Settings

In Settings, you can edit your organization (Users and Subscriptions settings), environment (Routes, Vehicles, Failure reason codes, Recipient notifications settings) and device settings.

**Organization** is a group of users who share a subscription and collaborate on data in one or more environments. **Environments** let you represent teams within a single company or provide separate spaces for testing and productive use.

Examples:

- A large company (organization) with smaller teams (environments) working on different projects.
- A holding company (organization) with smaller companies (environments) working in different spheres.
- A middle-size company (organization) with several depots (environments).

As a Free level user, you will have only 1 environment. If you want to use more environments for different purposes, **upgrade to Enterprise**.

Check more details about [Mobile App: Settings](mobile-app-settings.md).

## Organization and Environment

### Set up your organization

As a user, you can register and create your own organization to which you invite other users. You may also be invited to join other organizations. To [accept invitation](../web-based-hub/hub-accept-invitation.md), [sign in](mobile-app-sign-in/index.md) to the app using the email address to which the invitation has been sent.

![App 10.png](/attachments/190d4700-4149-4fd8-a737-42a23495a6fc.png)

If you are not invited to any organization, the default organization will be created once your account is registered. **Free level subscription** will be assigned to you, **no card required**. You will be redirected to Map page to start planning your routes.

![20261117_1.png](/attachments/mwuqdj5z-bx5t02us.png)

If you want to edit your organization and environment settings, press `Settings` in Menu. **Organization** is a group of users who share a subscription and collaborate on data in one or more environments. **Environments** let you represent teams within a single company or provide separate spaces for testing and productive use.

Examples:

- A large company (organization) with smaller teams (environments) working on different projects.
- A holding company (organization) with smaller companies (environments) working in different spheres.
- A middle-size company (organization) with several depots (environments).

As a Free level user, you will have only 1 environment. If you want to use more environments for different purposes, **upgrade to Enterprise**.

### Change Organization and Environment

To choose the organization to work in, you need to click on the `menu` icon at the top right corner of Map page, then click on the Organization. You will see the dialog where you have to choose the organization you need if you have several. After you choose the organization, you need to choose the environment if you have several. To close the dialog just click on any space on the page.

## Profile Information

When you register using an email and password or a mobile phone number, it will also be set as your display name. You can change it anytime in your Profile. To access your profile information, open the Menu and tap on your email address or display name. Remember to click the `Update` button to save any changes.
