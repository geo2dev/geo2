# Mobile app: AI Assistant

## Introduction

Geo2 AI assistant helps you manage routes, stops, deliveries, and daily operations directly from the Geo2 mobile app using natural language. Instead of navigating through multiple screens or entering information manually, you can simply ask Geo2 AI assistant to perform actions, find information, or explain how different features work.

You can interact with the assistant using text or voice, making it particularly useful when you are on the road and want to complete common tasks with fewer taps.

Geo2 AI assistant can help you:

* Create new routes and update existing ones
* Add stops using addresses, place names, or spoken requests
* Import stops from photos, screenshots, and PDFs
* Move stops between routes
* Optimize routes with or without required stop time windows
* Add, edit, and delete breaks
* Start and complete routes
* Find information about planned routes and assigned drivers
* Check route progress and delivery statuses
* Retrieve Proof of Delivery (POD) information
* Navigate to stops and different areas of the app
* Get step-by-step guidance on using Geo2 features

You can also ask follow-up questions. Geo2 AI assistant keeps the context of your conversation, so you do not need to repeat all the details with every request.

## What You Can Do with AI Assistant

Geo2 AI assistant supports a wide range of route management and delivery operations. You can use natural language rather than memorizing specific commands.

### Create and Manage Routes

Create new routes, update existing ones, change route details, and manage driver assignments.

For example:

* "Create a new route for tomorrow at 8 AM."
* "Create a route called Morning Deliveries."
* "Change tomorrow's route start time to 9 AM."
* "Assign John to Route 123."
* "Release all routes planned for tomorrow."
* "Show me my current route."

### Add Stops to Routes

Add individual or multiple stops by providing addresses, place names, or delivery details.

For example:

* "Add 1720 Main Street, Kansas City, MO to my route."
* "Add Union Station Kansas City as my next stop."
* "Add these three addresses to my current route."
* "Add a delivery stop for John Smith at 1100 Main St, Kansas City, MO."

You can also attach photos, screenshots, or PDFs containing delivery addresses and ask the Assistant to create stops from them.

### Move Stops Between Routes

Reassign individual stops or groups of stops between routes, including undelivered stops that need to be rescheduled.

For example:

* "Move stop 5 from Route 123 to Route 456."
* "Move these three stops to John's route."
* "Move all undelivered stops from Route 123 to a new route tomorrow at 8 AM."

### Optimize Routes

Optimize the order of stops to plan an efficient route. You can choose whether the optimization should consider required delivery time windows.

For example:

* "Optimize my current route."
* "Optimize Route 123 considering required delivery times."
* "Optimize this route without considering required stop times."

### Manage Breaks

Add breaks to your route, change their duration, or remove breaks that are no longer needed.

For example:

* "Add a 30-minute break after stop 15."
* "Change my lunch break to 45 minutes."
* "Delete the lunch break."

### Start and Complete routes

Manage route execution directly through the assistant.

For example:

* "Start my current route."
* "Start Route 123."
* "Start all routes assigned to John today."
* "Complete my current route."
* "Mark Route 123 as completed."

### Find Information about Planned Routes

Ask the assistant to find routes based on dates, assignments, or other route details.

For example:

* "What routes are planned for today?"
* "Show me tomorrow's routes."
* "Which routes are assigned to John today?"
* "Who is the driver for Route 123?"

### Check Route Progress and Stop Details

Get information about your current route, remaining stops, delivery instructions, and expected completion time.

For example:

* "What's my next stop?"
* "How many stops do I have left?"
* "What time am I expected to finish?"
* "What's the address of stop 12?"
* "Read the delivery instructions for my next stop."
* "How many stops are completed on Route 123?"
* "Which stops are still pending?"
* "Show me the details for order #5482."
* "Which route is order #5482 on?"

This is particularly useful during deliveries when you need to check information without manually searching through your route or stop list.

### Check Delivery Statuses and Proof of Delivery

Geo2 AI assistant can retrieve delivery status information and available Proof of Delivery (POD) details associated with your orders and stops.

You can ask about completed deliveries, failed deliveries, and specific delivery records.

For example:

* "Has order #5482 been delivered?"
* "Which deliveries failed today?"
* "Show me the undelivered stops from Route 123."
* "When was order #5482 delivered?"
* "Who completed the delivery for order #5482?"
* "Does this delivery have a signature?"
* "Are there any POD photos for this order?"
* "Show me the POD details for order #5482."

The assistant can help you find available information such as delivery status, completion date and time, assigned driver, delivery photos, and signatures.

The information provided depends on the data recorded for the relevant delivery.

### Navigate within the App

Use the assistant to open routes, stops, and different areas of Geo2 without manually navigating through the app.

For example:

* "Show me my current route."
* "Open stop 8."
* "Take me to my next delivery."
* "Navigate to my next stop."
* "Open the Routes page."
* "Take me to Vehicle settings."
* "Show me the details for order #5482."

When the assistant opens another screen, your conversation can remain active, allowing you to continue interacting with Geo2.

### Get Help with Geo2 features

Ask the Assistant how to use different Geo2 features or complete specific actions.

For example:

* "How do I create a route?"
* "How do I scan a package?"
* "How do I record a failed delivery?"
* "How do I add proof of delivery?"
* "How can I add a required delivery time?"
* "How do I create a vehicle check?"
* "Where can I manage my subscription?"

The assistant provides guidance to help you find the relevant features and complete tasks within Geo2.

## Import Stops Using AI Assistant

You can use Geo2 AI assistant to create stops from information you already have instead of entering every address manually.

The assistant supports importing delivery information from:

* Photos of printed delivery lists
* Screenshots containing addresses
* PDF documents with delivery information

To import stops:

1. Open Geo2 AI assistant in the mobile app.
2. Attach a photo, screenshot, or PDF containing the delivery information.
3. Enter a request describing what you want the Assistant to do.

For example:

* "Add these addresses to my current route."
* "Create stops from this delivery list."
* "Import all addresses from this screenshot."
* "Create a new route using the addresses in this PDF."

Geo2 AI assistant processes the attached file, extracts the relevant delivery information, and prepares an order draft.

Review the extracted information and tap `Confirm` on the order draft to create the orders (stops).

This is useful when working with printed manifests, screenshots from other delivery platforms, or digital delivery lists.

## AI Assistant Voice Mode

Voice mode lets you speak with Geo2 AI Assistant instead of typing requests. The assistant listens to your requests and responds using voice, allowing you to manage routes, find delivery information, navigate through the app, and receive guidance without typing.

Voice mode supports the same AI capabilities as text mode and is particularly useful when you are on the road.

### Start a Voice Conversation

To start using voice mode:

1. Open AI assistant in the Geo2 mobile app.
2. Tap the `speaker icon` next to the **Ask anything** field.
3. The assistant switches to voice mode.
4. Turn on your microphone and start speaking.

When voice mode starts, the screen displays **Ask me anything in voice** and provides microphone controls.

![20261120\_4.png](/attachments/mwoxwduv-n6670y65.png)

The assistant greets you and invites you to speak your request.

You do not need to memorize specific commands. Simply describe what you want to do using natural language.

For example:

* "What's my next delivery?"
* "Take me to my next stop."
* "Add a 20-minute break after this stop."
* "I have three addresses to add."
* "What time should I finish today?"
* "Show me tomorrow's route."
* "Which deliveries are still pending?"
* "Has my last delivery been completed?"

While using voice mode, Geo2 AI assistant replies using voice as well.

You can continue the conversation naturally and ask follow-up questions without repeating the full context.

### Suggested Requests

Geo2 AI assistant may display suggested requests based on how you use the app. You can select one of these suggestions instead of typing or speaking the request manually.

When you select a suggestion while voice mode is active, the request is sent as part of your current voice conversation, and Geo2 AI assistant responds using voice.

### Use AI Assistant while Navigating the App

Some requests require Geo2 AI assistant to open another screen.

For example, you might ask:

* "Show me my current route."
* "Open stop 8."
* "Take me to my next delivery."
* "Show me the details for order #5482."

When this happens, the full AI assistant screen collapses so you can interact with the relevant part of the app.

The assistant remains active as a **floating control** on the screen. If you are using voice mode, you can continue speaking with the Assistant and hearing its responses while navigating other screens in Geo2. Tap the floating AI Assistant control to return to the full Assistant screen and continue your conversation.

To close the active Assistant completely:

* Swipe it down, or
* Tap the `X` button.

After closing it, the regular AI assistant entry point is displayed again.

![20261120\_5.png](/attachments/mwoy05x8-5h0xizo8.png)

### Switch between Text and Voice

You can switch between text and voice depending on what is most convenient. Use text mode when you prefer typing requests or reviewing the conversation on screen. Use voice mode when you want to interact with the assistant by speaking and hearing its responses.

Geo2 AI assistant supports spoken requests and voice responses in supported languages. When possible, it responds in the same language you use. Speech recognition and voice availability may vary depending on the language.

## Limitations and Access

Geo2 AI assistant is available across Geo2 subscription levels, but certain capabilities and usage limits depend on your subscription.

* **Free and Pro:** Up to 10 AI requests per day.
* **Advanced and Enterprise:** Unlimited AI requests.
* **Order creation:** Free and Pro users can create one order at a time using the assistant. Bulk operations are available with Advanced and Enterprise subscriptions.

Geo2 AI assistant also respects your organization and environment permissions. The actions you can perform depend on your assigned user role. For example, users without permission to create or update routes and orders cannot perform these actions through the Assistant.

However, they can still use the assistant for permitted tasks, such as finding assigned routes, checking delivery details, and getting guidance on using Geo2 features.

Some requests may require additional information or confirmation before the assistant can complete the action.
