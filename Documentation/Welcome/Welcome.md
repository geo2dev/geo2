## About Geo2

> [!NOTE]
> **Reduce the financial and environmental cost of delivery.**

Geo2 is a **cloud-based transport management system (TMS)** and **all-in-one delivery route planner** designed to help businesses and independent drivers plan, execute, track, and optimize the movement of goods.

Using modern technology and AI-powered tools, Geo2 helps reduce delivery costs, shorten driving time, and simplify day-to-day delivery operations. Whether you are a solo driver or managing a growing logistics team, Geo2 provides everything you need to manage routes, track expenses, monitor vehicle conditions, and analyze operational performance efficiently.

It gives you the tools to:

- Reduce transport costs with automated route optimization
- Save time on route planning with AI-powered route creation and management
- Minimize delivery address errors with built-in address geocoding
- Enable fully electronic proof-of-delivery (POD) records
- Keep recipients informed with automated emails and tracking SMS
- Track vehicles and drivers in real time
- Track distance traveled with mileage tracking and detailed reports
- Calculate and analyze route costs based on vehicle expenses, distance, driving time, and driver costs
- Manage vehicle checks to monitor vehicle conditions and support fleet maintenance
- Analyze team performance through analytics charts, detailed reports, and an AI Assistant that provides insights into delivery operations and team efficiency

For an additional overview, visit **[geo2.com](https://geo2.com/)**.

All Geo2 data are encrypted both at rest and in transit.  For further details, please see **[Default encryption at rest](https://cloud.google.com/docs/security/encryption/default-encryption)** and **[Encryption in transit](https://cloud.google.com/docs/security/encryption-in-transit)**.
