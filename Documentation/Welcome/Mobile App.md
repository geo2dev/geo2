# Mobile App

The Geo2 mobile app is best suited for solo delivery drivers, including Amazon Flex, Walmart Spark, Evri, and other gig delivery drivers, as well as owners of local delivery businesses and drivers working for companies where route planning is managed by a transport manager or dispatcher in Geo2 Hub. The app supports route planning and optimization, stop management, and bulk stop creation through features such as Paste List (paste multiple addresses at once) and Import from photos (screenshots) and PDFs, allowing you to quickly create routes from printed or digital delivery lists. Additionally, the mobile app includes Proof of Delivery (POD) and vehicle check features to capture delivery confirmations and vehicle status at the start and completion of each route. While the mobile app enables you to create and optimize routes and manage stops on the go, Geo2 Hub provides advanced capabilities for larger-scale operations, including bulk data import via Excel (CSV), managing multiple routes on a single map, analytics and reporting, and AI-powered route building.

You can download the mobile app from [**App Store**](https://apps.apple.com/app/geo2/id1594180686) and [**Google Play**](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.geo2.app).

> [!CAUTION]
> ### Data and Costs
>
> The synchronization of high-resolution images - photos and signatures - as well as tracking are data-intensive.  Monitor mobile device data usage and consider either capping data usage in line with the mobile network provider contract or make use of "unlimited" data plans.  We cannot accept responsibility for any costs or losses incurred in the course of using the mobile app.
